# 30 / 60 / 90 Day Platform Rollout Plan

Audience: Platform team lead and Engineering VP sponsor.

## Days 1-30 - Foundation (Crawl -> Walk)

| # | Task | Owner | Outcome | Playbook reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Form platform team and agree operating model | Platform Eng Lead | Team charter, scope boundaries, and cadence agreed | `docs/adoption/platform-team-operating-model.md` |
| 2 | Bootstrap service registry for top 10 production services | Platform Eng + App Team Leads | Initial service inventory and ownership visible | `catalog/services/`, `docs/golden-paths/service-catalog.md` |
| 3 | Run workstation validation on all platform engineers | Platform Eng | 100% team readiness baseline | `scripts/env-checker.sh` |
| 4 | Stand up and verify Kind cluster on every platform engineer laptop | Platform Eng | Consistent inner-loop development baseline | `local-dev/kind/`, `docs/golden-paths/platform-onboarding.md` |
| 5 | Install and enforce pre-commit hooks on all platform repos | Platform Eng | Shift-left quality checks active | `.pre-commit-config.yaml`, `docs/guides/pre-commit-setup.md` |
| 6 | Configure OIDC federation for top 3 cloud accounts | Platform Eng + SecOps / GRC | Keyless auth path established | `docs/guides/github-actions-oidc.md` |
| 7 | Bootstrap remote Terraform state for production cloud account | Platform Eng | Shared state and locking for IaC | `terraform/_bootstrap/README.md` |
| 8 | Validate one full golden path end to end (recommend Kubernetes microservice) | Platform Eng + Pilot App Team | One production-like delivery path proven | `docs/golden-paths/kubernetes-microservice.md` |
| 9 | Install Kyverno on non-production cluster in Audit mode | Platform Eng + SecOps / GRC | Policy signal baseline without blocking | `policy/kyverno/`, `docs/decisions/ADR-004-policy-enforcement-layering.md` |
| 10 | Deploy Prometheus stack and define at least one SLO per pilot service | Platform Eng + App Team | Reliability baseline and alert visibility | `observability/prometheus/`, `docs/golden-paths/slo-driven-development.md` |
| 11 | Ensure repo quality gate passes on the playbook fork | Platform Eng | Governance gate integrated into CI | `.github/workflows/repo-quality.yml` |

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Playbook assets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Policy enforcement introduced too quickly causes delivery friction | Medium | High | Use Audit-first graduation and exception process with expiry | `docs/decisions/ADR-004-policy-enforcement-layering.md`, `docs/adoption/exception-waiver-process.md` |
| Platform team becomes a bottleneck for onboarding and approvals | Medium | High | Publish RACI, SLAs, and self-service paths | `docs/adoption/raci.md`, `docs/adoption/platform-team-operating-model.md` |
| Compliance evidence remains manual and inconsistent | High | Medium | Automate CronJobs and scripted evidence collection | `docs/golden-paths/compliance-reporting.md`, `secops/compliance/scripts/collect-evidence.sh` |
| Reliability targets are defined but not operationalized | Medium | High | Enforce SLO cadence, runbook ownership, and review forum | `docs/runbooks/slo-quarterly-review.md`, `docs/golden-paths/slo-driven-development.md` |
| FinOps adoption stalls due to weak accountability | Medium | Medium | Assign FinOps ownership and tie PR loop to monthly review | `finops/config/budgets.yaml`, `docs/golden-paths/finops-optimization.md` |
